Problem Statement and Motivation
Underwater images suffer from multiple distortions:
Color distortion
 (loss of red, dominance of blue/green) 
Low contrast
 due to light attenuation 
Haze and blur
 caused by scattering 
Noise
 from suspended particles
 Main Causes:
Light absorption (red absorbed quickly) 
Light scattering (reduces clarity)

--- SLIDE ppt/slides/slide5.xml ---
Objectives
To identify the major challenges in underwater images such as color distortion, low contrast, and reduced visibility caused by light absorption and scattering in water.
To analyze existing image enhancement techniques and machine learning approaches used for improving underwater image quality.
To develop a machine learning-based model that enhances underwater images by improving color balance, contrast, and visibility.
To evaluate the performance of the proposed model using image quality metrics such as PSNR and SSIM by comparing original and enhanced images.

System Architecture
Dataset Collection
Underwater image datasets such as UIEB(Underwater Image Enhancement Benchmark)are collected to train and test the enhancement model under different underwater conditions.
Image Preprocessing
Resize Images : Images are resized to a fixed dimension (e.g., 256×256) to ensure uniform input for the model.
Noise Removal : Filtering techniques are applied to remove noise caused by underwater particles and lighting conditions.
Normalization : Pixel values are scaled between 0 and 1 to improve training efficiency and model performance.

--- SLIDE ppt/slides/slide17.xml ---
Result Analysis and Discussions
U-Net achieved the highest PSNR (19.72) and SSIM (0.844), showing superior enhancement performance. 
CNN provided moderate enhancement with acceptable image reconstruction quality. 
Autoencoder reduced underwater noise effectively but preserved fewer texture details. 
Enhanced outputs showed better visibility, improved colour balance, and higher contrast compared to original underwater images. 
Experimental results prove that deep learning models perform better than traditional underwater enhancement techniques.

Future Scope
Potential future enhancement focus would be on the investigation of deeper learning methods used to develop a smaller architecture to run on small-scale portable devices as well as on systems used to operate in a lower power underwater environment. Lightweight models would enable deployment in underwater drones and portable marine monitoring systems.
Advanced artificial intelligence techniques such as
:
  Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) 
  Vision Transformers
  Diffusion Models  
  Reinforcement Learning 
can be explored to further improve underwater image restoration and visual quality.
